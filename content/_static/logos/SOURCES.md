# Dark-background logo assets

The five assets listed below are publisher-supplied white/negative variants.
No CSS colour filters are applied. Original light-mode assets are unchanged.

- `cc3/eurohpc-dark.svg`: [EuroHPC JU official logo pack](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/media-events/media/visuals_en), `SVG/EuroHPC JU logo monochrome NEGATIVE SVG-01.svg`.
- `partners/liu-dark.svg`: [LiU web distribution](https://staff.gitlab-pages.liu.se/dist/global/images/logo-main-en.svg), English white logo.
- `partners/rise-dark.svg`: white SVG embedded as `img.logo` in the [RISE website](https://www.ri.se/sv/om-rise/nyheter-och-press/pressbilder).
- `national/vr-dark.png`: [Swedish Research Council logo pack](https://www.vr.se/om-vetenskapsradet/press.html), `Internationell logotyp/Standing/White/logotyp_vetenskapsrådet_stående_ENG_RGB_500px-vit.png`.
- `national/vinnova-dark.svg`: [Vinnova official logo pack](https://www.vinnova.se/en/m/newsroom/logos/), `Logotyper/Digital/English/svg/1-vinnova-swedens-innovation-agency-white.svg`.

Retrieved 2026-10-09.

## Unofficial design previews

- `cc3/hpc-in-europe-dark-preview.svg`: local adaptation of `hpc-in-europe.svg`;
  only the lettering fill changes from `#383838` to white. Gold stars (`#ffde59`),
  paths and dimensions are unchanged.
- `cc3/eurocc3-dark-preview.svg`: local adaptation of `eurocc3.svg`; only the
  dark fill and stroke (`#444`) change to white. Paths and dimensions are unchanged.
